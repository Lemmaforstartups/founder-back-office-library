# Founder back-office library

Free guides, templates and tools for running the back office of a lean AI startup, seed to Series B: finance, operations and investor reporting. Free to read and use, no sign-up.

Maintained by [Lemma for Startups](https://lemmaforstartups.com/). Web version: https://lemmaforstartups.github.io/founder-back-office-library/

## Templates and tools

- [Monthly investor update template](https://lemmaforstartups.com/templates/investor-update/): Copy-ready: headline, cash, burn, runway, asks, wins and misses.
- [All templates](https://lemmaforstartups.com/templates/)
- [Burn rate and runway calculator](https://lemmaforstartups.com/tools/burn-rate-calculator/): Net burn, runway, the month cash runs out, compute share of spend. Runs in your browser.
- [Startup budget template with runway](https://lemmaforstartups.com/guides/startup-budget-template-with-runway/): 18-month budget for Excel and Google Sheets.
- [Seed stage board deck template](https://lemmaforstartups.com/guides/seed-board-deck-template/): 10 slides for PowerPoint and Google Slides.
